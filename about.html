About this Page

The Commonplace is a running archive of essays, organized the way an old commonplace book was — a personal notebook where ideas get sorted by theme rather than by date, so what you were thinking about history sits together, and what you were thinking about code sits together, regardless of when it was written.

It holds writing across eight sections: History, Philosophy, Economy, Programming, Culture, Local Politics, Global Politics, and Weather.

Who writes it

The Commonplace is written and maintained by Glenn (PoeticGlenn), a solo developer and writer based in Diplahan, Zamboanga Sibugay, Philippines.

How it's built

This is a fully offline-capable web app — everything you write is stored directly in your browser, with no account and no server-side database. That keeps it simple and keeps your writing private to your own device.
