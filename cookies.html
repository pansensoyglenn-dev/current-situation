Cookies

Last updated: August 25,2026

The Commonplace itself does not set any cookies. Your writing is stored using your browser's IndexedDB, a separate local-storage technology that never leaves your device and is not shared with the site's author.

Third-party cookies

This site displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may set cookies in your browser to serve and measure ads, including for personalization based on your browsing activity across sites. These cookies are controlled by Google, not by The Commonplace.

You can review or opt out of this at Google Ads Settings, and most browsers let you block third-party cookies entirely in their privacy settings.

Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may log the request (including your IP address) as part of serving the font files. See Google's privacy policy for details.
