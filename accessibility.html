Accessibility

Last updated: 2026

The Commonplace aims to be usable by as many people as possible, including people using screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, or browser text-size adjustments.

What's in place

  • Semantic HTML structure (headings, landmarks, labeled form fields).
  • A dark color palette chosen with contrast in mind for comfortable reading.
  • Text that scales with your browser's zoom and font-size settings rather than fixed pixel sizing throughout.
  • Buttons and links sized for touch and keyboard use, with visible focus states.
  • Alt text on photos where a title is available to describe them.

Known limitations

Videos attached to entries don't currently have captions or transcripts, since they're personal recordings rather than produced content. If a specific entry's video content matters to you and you need it described in text, reach out and the author can help. The app is built and tested primarily on modern mobile browsers (Safari and Chrome); older or less common browsers may render it imperfectly.

Feedback
