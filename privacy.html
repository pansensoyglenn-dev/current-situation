Data Privacy

Last updated: 2026

What this page covers

This is a personal site with no user accounts and no server-side database. Almost everything described here follows from that one fact.

What you write

Entries, photos, and videos you create in this app are stored only in your own browser's local storage on the device you're using (a technology called IndexedDB). Nothing you write is uploaded to a server, because this site doesn't have one. If you clear your browser's site data, or use a different device or browser, that content won't be there — there's no account to sync it back from.

Sharing

The Share button on an entry opens your device's own native share sheet (or, as a fallback, lets you download the media and copy a caption). Nothing is sent anywhere automatically — sharing only happens when you actively choose an app to share to.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and to measure ad performance. This site itself does not control that data collection. You can learn more and adjust your ad personalization settings at Google's advertising policy page and Google Ads Settings.

Fonts

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which involves your browser making a request to Google's servers. Google's handling of that request is covered by their own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, in line with the above (there's simply nothing collected server-side to begin with).

Questions
