Data Privacy

Last updated: [DATE]

The Commonplace is built so your writing never leaves your device. Everything you write — titles, entries, photos, and video — is stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage. No account, login, or server-side database is used, and Glenn Pansensoy (the site's author) cannot read, access, or recover anything you write here.

What stays on your device

  • Article titles, body text, and categories
  • Any photo or video you attach to an entry
  • Your search history within the app (never transmitted)

Because this data lives only in your browser, clearing your browser data, switching devices, or uninstalling your browser will permanently delete it. Use the "Export as JSON" or "Export as HTML" buttons on the home screen to back up your writing.
